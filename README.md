# Fizz promo codes in Canada

A plain guide to how Fizz mobile works in Canada and how to use a Fizz promo or referral code. Kept up to date by the team behind [FizzPromoCodes.ca](https://fizzpromocodes.ca/fr/codes-parrainage-fizz-regions-2026/).

## Can you sign up for Fizz?

Fizz checks your home address when you subscribe. If your address is in an area where Fizz is offered, you can open a plan. The check happens during sign-up, so you can see the result right away.

## Coverage area vs subscription area

- Subscription area: where you must live to open a plan.
- Coverage area: where your calls, texts and data are included. You pick it when you build your plan: your province only, all of Canada, or Canada plus the United States. You can change it later and the change applies at your next billing cycle.

## Does a code work for every customer in Canada?

Codes are entered once, when you activate your first mobile plan, in the single "promo or referral code" field. Only one code can be used per plan, and some offers depend on your region and on a minimum plan amount.
